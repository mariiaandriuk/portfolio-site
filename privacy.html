Privacy policy.

This notice describes the current portfolio website and its private editing area.

Who to contact

This portfolio is managed by Maria Andriiuk. For questions about your personal information, email mariia.andriiuk0105@gmail.com.

Contact enquiries

The email link opens your own mail application. It does not send a message or submit a form through this website. If you choose to write, your email address, name, message and any attachments are processed through the email provider to respond to your enquiry and discuss a potential project. Please share only information relevant to your request.

Correspondence is used to answer enquiries and, when requested, take steps towards a service agreement. Relevant records may also be needed to meet legal obligations or resolve a dispute. Correspondence should be retained only while needed for those purposes; a fixed automatic deletion period has not been configured by this website.

Sign-in and the private editor

The site is hosted on OpenAI Sites. Where sign-in is required, OpenAI handles authentication. The application receives a signed-in user identifier and email address to check access to the private editor. The editor stores portfolio text, image files, file metadata, and the identifier of the account that saved or uploaded them. Visitors cannot edit the portfolio.

Portfolio records and uploaded images are retained for managing the site. Removing an image from a project removes its reference from that project; it does not immediately erase the stored file. Contact the address above about removal of personal information.

Technical data and cookies

The portfolio records daily totals of page views, project openings and email-link clicks to understand interest in the work. These totals store only the UTC day, event type, page or project identifier and count. The analytics code does not store IP addresses, visitor identifiers, referrers or browser fingerprints, and does not use analytics cookies or browser storage. Signed-in owner activity, recognized automated user agents and requests with Do Not Track enabled are excluded. Daily aggregate totals are retained without an automatic expiry. The portfolio does not include advertising pixels, a mailing-list form or marketing cookies. Hosting and sign-in services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and security logs, and may use cookies needed to deliver or protect their services. This notice does not claim that the hosting platform collects no data.

OpenAI describes its own processing, retention and international data handling in its privacy policy.

External services

Behance and any other linked social profiles open on external websites. Their providers apply their own privacy policies after you follow a link. Messages sent to the listed Gmail address are handled through Google’s email service; see Google’s privacy policy. These providers may process information in countries outside your own.

Your choices and requests

You may ask about access, correction or deletion of your personal information, and about restricting or objecting to its use, where applicable under the law. Email mariia.andriiuk0105@gmail.com. You may also raise a concern with the relevant data-protection authority. Requests concerning your OpenAI, Google or Behance account should be directed to that provider.

Changes to this notice

This notice will need to reflect any changes to the site’s data use, including the addition of enquiry forms, mailing lists or analytics. The update date is shown above.
