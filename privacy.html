This notice explains what happens when you contact me through this portfolio.

Who to contact

Contact enquiries

When you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address and message. The form sends these details through Web3Forms to my Gmail inbox so I can reply to your enquiry and discuss a possible project. Please do not include sensitive information in your message.
