05

How a project runs

  1. Brief. Tell me about the project: location, stage, area, zones, references and budget. English or Ukrainian.
  2. Scope and fee. We agree what is included, the price, timing and revision rounds, in writing, before work starts.
  3. Start. 30% upfront.
  4. Concept development. Idea and story, guest journey, mood, materials and light.
  5. Presentation and revisions. The zone and small venue concepts include two revision rounds; for other items they are agreed with the scope.
  6. Handover. The remaining 70% is due on delivery. You receive the agreed presentation and files.
  7. Drawings and 3D, if in your scope. Ordered together with the concept as a combined package, or separately, and confirmed in writing. They are produced by my specialists and are not construction-approved documents.
  8. Creative direction during construction, if you need it. A separate agreement.

Timing: a compact concept of my own work, without specialists, takes from about 3 to 4 days, depending on scope. Larger projects are timed after the brief. Competitor or site analysis can be added if needed.

Discounts may be available for corporate orders. Terms are agreed individually after the brief. Final fee and timeline are confirmed in writing after a short brief covering the area, zones and budget. Concept stage only. The concepts on this site are unbuilt.
