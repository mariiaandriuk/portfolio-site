How a project runs

01Project briefTell me about the project: location, stage, area, zones, references and budget. English or Ukrainian.
02Scope and feeWe agree what is included, the price, timing and revision rounds, in writing, before work starts.
03Start30% upfront.
04Concept developmentIdea and story, guest journey, mood, materials and light.
05Presentation and revisionsThe zone and small venue concepts include two revision rounds; for other items they are agreed with the scope.
06HandoverThe remaining 70% is due on delivery. You receive the agreed presentation and files.
07Drawings and 3D, if in your scopeOrdered together with the concept as a combined package, or separately, and confirmed in writing. They are done by specialists I involve, under my coordination, and are not construction-approved documents.
08Creative direction during construction, if you need itA separate agreement.

Timing: a compact concept of my own work, without specialists, takes from about 3 to 4 days, depending on scope. Larger projects are timed after the project brief. Competitor or site analysis can be added if needed.

Discounts may be available for corporate orders. Terms are agreed individually after the project brief. Final fee and timeline are confirmed in writing after the project brief, which covers the area, zones and budget. Concept stage only. The concepts on this site are unbuilt.
