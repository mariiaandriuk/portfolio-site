Residential concept study

BEDROOM CONCEPTS

Six bedrooms, six moods — a study of style and atmosphere.

Sculptural Organic LuxuryOpen full size ↗

BEDROOM CONCEPTS is a study of how far atmosphere can stretch within a single room type. Six master-bedroom concepts — Sculptural Organic Luxury, European Maximalism, Burgundy Noir, Wabi Sabi Zen, Neo Deco Luxury and Western Gothic Luxury — each with its own material palette, lighting scenario and emotional register. From soft organic minimalism to collected maximalism and dark gothic warmth, the study shows the same method behind every project: a clear idea, honest materials and light that tells the story.

Material direction
Each concept carries its own palette — from travertine, limewash and bouclé to walnut, velvet, marble, brass and natural stone. Final finishes and construction details would be developed during technical design.

My role
Concept design, creative direction and visual storytelling — Maria Andriiuk.

Concept & atmosphere

One room, six worlds. Each bedroom is a complete mood — serene and organic, richly layered, dark and sensual, wabi-sabi calm, deco glamour or gothic warmth — designed as a coherent, livable whole.

Signature elements

Sculptural curved headboards and arched portals; layered textiles and collected objects; fireplaces and chandeliers; material palettes built from stone, wood, velvet, brass and linen.

Lighting direction

Layered warm light in every concept: coves and concealed strips, sculptural pendants and chandeliers, candle-like accents. Scenes sit around 2700-3000K for intimacy and depth.

Scope of work

Concept design and creative direction; six bedroom atmospheres with material and lighting direction; visual storytelling and presentation boards.
