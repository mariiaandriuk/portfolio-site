Immersive photography studio concept

ELEMENTS

Five natural worlds. One creative destination.

Botanical StudioOpen full size ↗

ELEMENTS is an immersive photography studio park concept with five distinct environments: Botanical, Desert, Stone, Air and Water. Each offers its own material palette, light and atmosphere within one creative destination. The concept treats the space itself as part of the image. Instead of relying only on temporary backdrops, it explores complete settings for fashion, portrait, product and brand shoots, with a variety of visual moods for different productions.

Material direction
Botanical: foliage, mineral finishes and timber. Desert: sand-toned plaster, clay and woven fibres. Stone: rough mineral surfaces. Air: pale textiles and translucent drapery. Water: glass, reflective finishes and blue-toned surfaces.

My role
Concept design, creative direction and visual storytelling — Maria Andriiuk.

Concept & atmosphere

Five natural worlds within one studio destination. The environments offer contrasting settings while sharing an emphasis on texture, atmosphere and carefully composed views.

Signature elements

Botanical planting; sand-toned arches; sculptural stone forms; flowing translucent fabrics; blue reflective surfaces. Each environment has a recognisable palette and focal composition.

The five studios

Botanical Layered greenery, deep emerald tones and filtered light create an intimate setting for portraits, beauty and lifestyle imagery. Desert Sand tones, arches, clay textures and woven elements create a warm setting for fashion, lifestyle and product shoots. Stone Raw mineral surfaces and sculptural forms create strong texture and shadow for portraiture, fashion and conceptual imagery. Air Pale textiles, flowing drapery and soft light create a light, quiet setting for movement, bridal, beauty and lifestyle photography. Water Blue tones, reflective finishes and water-like light patterns create a cool, dreamlike setting for beauty, product and editorial work.

Lighting direction

The lighting direction changes between environments: filtered light in Botanical, golden warmth in Desert, pronounced shadows in Stone, diffused daylight in Air, and reflections or projected patterns in Water.

Experience & journey

Photographers and creative teams choose an environment to suit a brief, then explore alternative angles, styling and light within it. The five settings support variation across a larger shoot or campaign.

Scope of work

Core concept and narrative; spatial atmosphere; material and colour direction; lighting mood; experience sequence; concept views and presentation.
