Warm minimalist interior concept

Terra House

Golden light, curved forms and quiet warmth — a home shaped around softness.

Living room in golden lightOpen full size ↗

Terra House is a warm minimalist interior concept built around golden light and curved, sculpted forms. Lime plaster walls, walnut joinery and travertine surfaces create one continuous, calm interior, while a sweeping curved staircase gives the home its quiet centre. The concept explores minimalism as warmth rather than absence: fewer elements, each given weight. Light moves across the plaster like a sundial through the day, and every room is composed around a single gesture — a curve, a shadow, a framed patch of garden.

Material direction
Warm lime plaster; travertine; walnut cabinetry and wall panels; dark marble accents; woven jute and wool; bouclé and linen upholstery; aged bronze details. Final finishes and construction details would be developed during technical design.

My role
Concept design, creative direction and visual storytelling — Maria Andriiuk.

Concept & atmosphere

A minimal residence where warmth comes from material and light rather than decoration. Rounded edges, deep window reveals and a restrained palette create rooms that feel calm, soft and permanently sunlit.

Signature elements

A sculptural curved staircase; deep plastered openings; a monolithic travertine kitchen island; woven jute textures; integrated walnut joinery; soft golden lighting concealed within shelves and niches.

Lighting direction

Low, warm and indirect. Grazing light across plaster reveals its texture, concealed lines beneath shelves and cabinetry replace visible fixtures, and daylight is treated as the main feature, filtered through deep reveals.

Experience & journey

Arrival moves through a calm hallway towards the curved stair, the home’s sculptural heart. Living and kitchen spaces flow together in a warm open plan, while the bedroom and stone bathing room turn inward for privacy and rest.

Scope of work

Overall concept and narrative; interior atmosphere; material and colour direction; lighting mood; room concepts; visual storytelling and presentation.
