Residential interior concept

NEO ART DECO

An emerald sanctuary — deep green, gold brass and botanical light in a modern deco language.

The emerald living roomOpen full size ↗

NEO ART DECO is a residential interior concept that reworks the glamour of art deco through nature. Deep emerald fluted panels, verde marble and brushed brass form the structure, while backlit botanical resin panels bring living light into every room — an emerald sanctuary with a calm, contemporary rhythm. The concept carries one palette through living room, kitchen, bedroom and bathroom: dark green and marble grounded by walnut and travertine, softened by bouclé and sheer linen, and warmed everywhere by layered golden light.

Material direction
Deep emerald fluted panels; verde and black marble; brushed gold brass; dark walnut; travertine; bouclé upholstery; botanical resin backlit panels; warm LED lighting. Final finishes and construction details would be developed during technical design.

My role
Concept design, creative direction and visual storytelling — Maria Andriiuk.

Concept & atmosphere

A sanctuary of elegance and calm. The deco vocabulary — fluting, arches, brass lines and symmetrical lighting — is kept restrained and modern, while botanical light panels give each room a living focal point and a connection to nature.

Signature elements

Backlit botanical resin panels; deep emerald fluted walls; a verde marble kitchen island and bathroom vanity; arched mirrors with brass frames; globe pendant clusters; a moon-like botanical medallion above the bed.

Lighting direction

Warm and layered: concealed LED lines along ceiling coves and shelving, globe pendants in soft clusters, picture-light accents on brass, and the glow of the botanical panels as ambient light. Light is used to graze texture rather than flood the room.

Experience & journey

The living room sets the mood — emerald, brass and soft bouclé. The kitchen turns the same palette functional around a marble island, the bedroom becomes the calmest chamber under its botanical medallion, and the bathroom closes the sequence as a dark, tranquil spa.

Scope of work

Overall concept and narrative; interior atmosphere; material and colour direction; lighting mood; room concepts; visual storytelling and presentation.
